Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

I specialize in full-stack web development, including frontend development with Angular/React, backend development with Spring Boot/Laravel, mobile app development, and UI/UX design. I also offer consultation on project architecture and technology stack selection.

What's your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity and scope. A simple website typically takes 2-4 weeks, while more complex web applications can take 2-3 months. I always provide detailed project timelines during the planning phase.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes, I work with clients both locally and internationally. I'm experienced in remote collaboration using tools like Slack, Zoom, and project management platforms to ensure smooth communication throughout the project.

What technologies do you specialize in?

I specialize in JavaScript, Java, PHP, and Python. For frameworks, I work with Angular, React, Spring Boot, Laravel, and Django. I'm also experienced with databases like PostgreSQL and MySQL, and deployment tools like Docker and Vercel.
